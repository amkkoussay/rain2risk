# Rain2Risk — Brand Identity (field-journal edition)

**Tagline:** *See the rain. Understand the risk.*
**Descriptor:** *Location intelligence for rainfall risk.*

## The idea

A field instrument, not a dashboard. Rain2Risk is a cartographer's notebook:
paper, ink, and one honest reading per location.

> Rain ↓ Terrain ↓ Land cover ↓ Map context ↓ Risk signal

## Logo — adopted as-is

The mark is the droplet of topographic contours over the risk grid,
on its own black plate (`frontend/assets/logo-mark.png`).
The full lockup (`frontend/assets/logo-lockup.png`) is shown as
"Fig. 01" — a printed plate, never redrawn, never recolored.

## Color — paper & ink

No blue gradients. No neon. Borders are ink hairlines; the black logo
plate is the darkest object on the page by design.

## Typography — the distinctive voice

- **Fraunces** (display) — headlines, the big score, the wordmark feel.
  Old-print character with optical sizing; italics for emphasis.
- **IBM Plex Mono** (data) — coordinates, eyebrows, labels, quality chips.
  The instrument voice.
- **Inter** (body) — paragraphs and UI text.

## Graphic language

Ruled-paper background, ink hairlines, dashed selection frames,
"Fig. 01 / NOTE 01" annotations, stamp-like pills. Map tiles stay
light — the chart on the desk.

## Voice

Short. Calm. Factual. Field notes, not marketing.

- "Four data layers. One local risk snapshot."
- "When data is missing it writes *unavailable*, never a guess."
- Readings are numbered (Nº 001, 002…) — each analysis is a recorded entry.
